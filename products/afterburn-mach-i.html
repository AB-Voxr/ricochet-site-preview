Every dose on the label.

275 mgCaffeine, 2 forms

Caffeine anhydrous plus sustained-release caffeine: fast onset and a long tail, roughly three cups of coffee per serving.

250 mgGreen Tea Extract

Standardized to 50 percent polyphenols and 15 percent EGCG, the catechins that pair with caffeine.

200 mgAcetyl-L-Carnitine

The carnitine form that also crosses into the brain, alongside 25 mg ProGBB, its precursor.
