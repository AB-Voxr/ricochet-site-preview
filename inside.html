Creatine Questions

Asked constantly, answered straight

Do I need a loading phase?

No. Five grams a day fills muscle creatine stores in three to four weeks on its own. Loading (about 20 g a day for five to seven days) just gets you there in a week; the end state is the same. Pick whichever your stomach prefers.

When should I take it?

Any time, every day, including rest days. Timing matters far less than never missing. Most people drop it in their Ballistic scoop before training or in water with breakfast.

Will it make me hold water?

Creatine pulls water into the muscle cell, which is part of how it works, so a small early rise on the scale (a pound or two) is normal. That water is inside the muscle, not under the skin.

Why unflavored?

So it disappears into whatever you already drink, and so the tub holds nothing but creatine: no flavor system, no sweeteners, no dyes. Other ingredients: none, and that's printed on the label.

Is it safe to take every day?

Creatine monohydrate is the most studied sports supplement in existence, with decades of research in healthy adults at 3 to 5 g a day. If you have a kidney condition, are pregnant or nursing, or take medication, talk to your doctor first, as the label says.

The thermogenic core

Caffeine is the most studied thermogenic ingredient there is: it raises alertness and energy expenditure, and it's why two capsules can carry a session. 250 mgGreen Tea Extract

Catechins that work with the caffeine

Standardized to 50 percent polyphenols and 15 percent EGCG, the catechin most of the green-tea research centers on. Green tea catechins and caffeine are the most studied thermogenic pairing in the category, each supporting the other's effect on energy expenditure.
