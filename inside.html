6,000 mgL-Citrulline

The pump engine

Citrulline converts to arginine and drives nitric oxide production, which widens blood vessels and pushes more blood into working muscle. That's the pump, the vascularity, and the nutrient delivery. First isolated from watermelon, which is still its most famous natural source.

Six grams is a full clinical-range dose. Plenty of brands run two or three and hide it in a blend.

300 mgDual-Source Caffeine

The energy curve, engineered

Two sources on purpose: Caffeine Anhydrous hits fast for onset, and Infinergy Di-Caffeine Malate releases slower for the long tail. One smooth curve through a full session instead of a spike and a wall.

This structure is the mechanical answer to the crash complaint that dominates the category.

Alpha-GPCCholine Source

Mind-to-muscle connection

A highly bioavailable choline source that supports acetylcholine, the neurotransmitter your nervous system uses to fire muscle. Lifters know it as the "dialed in" feeling: sharper focus on the working muscle when the set gets heavy.

L-TyrosineAmino Acid

Focus under stress

Tyrosine is a building block for dopamine and norepinephrine, the chemicals that keep you locked in under fatigue and stress. Found naturally in protein foods like eggs and almonds, dosed here so it's there when concentration normally slips, late in the session.

Pink SaltHimalayan

Hydration and fullness

Sodium drives fluid into the blood and muscle cells. A measured dose of Himalayan salt supports hydration, performance in sweaty sessions, and fuller pumps working alongside the citrulline.

Formula Questions

Asked constantly, answered straight

Why 300 mg of caffeine and not 400?

Because the goal is a session you finish strong, not a heart rate you have to manage. 300 mg split across two release speeds covers hard training without the wreckage that pushes people off high-stim pres entirely.

Will I get the tingles?

Yes. Ballistic runs a full 3,200 mg of Beta-Alanine, so the tingles are part of the deal, and they mean the dose is real. They fade as your body adapts; start with a half scoop if you're new to it.

Can I stack Ballistic with Creatine?

Yes, that's the intended entry stack. Creatine is a daily foundation; Ballistic is the session tool. Take creatine any time of day, Ballistic 20 to 30 minutes before training.

Is it third-party tested?

Every batch is produced in an FDA-registered, GMP-compliant American facility. Published third-party certification is on our roadmap, and lab sheets will live on this page as they land.

Who should not take it?

High-stim pre-workouts aren't for everyone. If you're sensitive to caffeine, pregnant or nursing, or on medication, talk to your doctor first. Always start with a half scoop to assess tolerance.
