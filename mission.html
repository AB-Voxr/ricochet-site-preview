Where It Started

The supply problem

Deployed service members train hard in some of the worst conditions on earth, and getting supplements you could actually trust out there was close to impossible. Long shipping waits. Sketchy third-party sellers. Labels you couldn't verify and had to take on faith.

Our founders lived that gap. When they came home, they built the brand they couldn't get: formulas made exclusively in the United States, in an FDA-registered, GMP-compliant Texas facility, with every dose printed on the label.

The Pledge

Care packages, downrange, at no cost

The mission is written into the business: fitness care packages shipped at no cost to deployed service members, wherever duty takes them. Every order placed on this store helps fund that supply line.

No service member should be limited in their pursuit of strength, health, and resilience because of where they're stationed. That's the standard, and it's why the brand says Redirect Your Health and means it.
